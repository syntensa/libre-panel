Your panel.

Free, open-source software for the small USB system-monitor displays sold as Turing Smart Screen, TURZX, WeAct, XuanFang and under many other names. Pick a theme or build your own, and see your PC at a glance.

Windows · Linux · macOS — GPL-3.0 — no account, no cloud, no vendor app

Fits your machine

One theme, every PC

Themes show the names your own CPU and GPU report, the weather for the place in your config and dates in English or German. Where a sensor is missing, its section steps aside: Studio shows a calendar sheet when the weather is off, and the disk on a PC without GPU readings. No editing needed.

Modules

Build your own in two minutes

Snap a theme together from modules on your panel's grid. They work like widgets on a phone's home screen: the larger, the more they show.

1

Pick a layout and a look

New offers starting layouts made for your panel, in six looks.

2

Drag modules onto the panel

Pick from 25 modules: hardware, clocks, weather, music, your calendar and more. Free cells light up; drop a module on another to swap them.

3

Pull a corner

One cell shows the load; two add the processor's name, temperature and power; three a history graph.

The weather does the same: given room, it shows the next days or hours (or both), as columns when long or big, as rows when tall. A big calendar shows the month.

4

Try another look

Colours, cards, corners, glow and fonts of every module change in one click.

5

On every panel

The grid follows the panel and keeps clear of edges the bezel hides. Switch the panel and the modules move along.

25 modules

From every core to your next appointment

Each module shows more the larger it is, steps aside when its readings are missing, and costs nothing while it is not on the panel.

Hardware

Every temperature and fan, the load of every core, every drive with its free space, the busiest programs, IP address and ping, the battery, and your game's frame rate with its 1% lows (PresentMon, Windows).

Time and the sky

Digital and analog clocks for any time zone, a world clock, countdowns to any day, sunrise, sunset and the moon's phase, computed offline for your place.

Your things

What is playing with its cover, the next events of your calendars, Home Assistant and MQTT values on a dashboard, and your pictures as a slideshow.

Built in

Seven themes, from 0.96" to 9.2"

Glow, soft gradients, smooth history graphs, 35 line icons with live weather symbols, and values that glide instead of jumping. Every theme moves to any other panel size in one click.

Theme editor

Design it in your browser

The editor renders with the same engine that drives the panel, so what you see is what you get.

  • Module library with live pictures in your look
  • Drag, drop, swap and resize modules on the grid
  • Free editing of every widget, with snapping and alignment guides
  • Undo and redo, palettes, bundled fonts, glow, gradients, colour rules
  • Every panel from a menu, portrait or landscape
  • Show on panel sends it there
Under the hood

Small screen, serious engine

50 fps video mode

On TURZX USB panels, frames go through the panel's own video decoder: graphs scroll and numbers glide.

Sensors

Every core, drive, temperature and fan, network and battery everywhere; on Windows also GPU and power via LibreHardwareMonitor. Calendars, Home Assistant and MQTT too.

Weather

From Open-Meteo for your location. Off by default, no API key.

Runs in the tray

Theme, brightness, pause and quit from the tray; starts with the system if you want; finds the panel when it is plugged in.

Safe to share

Themes are plain JSON plus images and fonts. They cannot run code or read files outside their folder.

Plugins

Sensor sources, widget types, display drivers, screens drawn in code and editor pages, installed with pip.

Install

Windows 10 / 11

Setup for your user, no admin rights; optional start at login.

Setup .exe

macOS 11+

Apple silicon. Drag to Applications; lives in the menu bar.

Disk image .dmg

Linux

x86-64 AppImage: make it executable and start it. A tarball too.

AppImage
# or with Python
pipx install "libre-panel[usb,serial,tray] @ git+https://github.com/syntensa/libre-panel@v0.4.1"
libre-panel tray        # background app with tray icon; opens the editor
libre-panel doctor      # check your panel, write a report

The downloads are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen asks once (More info → Run anyway); on macOS open the app the first time with right-click → Open. Close the vendor app first; it keeps the panel to itself.
