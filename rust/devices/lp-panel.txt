lp-panel: a panel tried with Libre Panel's driver in Rust

Close the panel's own program first (for the MAJESTY 9.16": MAJESTY AIO MONITOR), as it
holds the panel's port. Then, in a terminal in this folder:

  lp-panel list
      the serial ports and what is on them; 33c3:7792 is the MAJESTY 9.16"

  lp-panel test
      the panel found by its USB ids, opened, and tried:
      1. a card for 5 s: top left red, top right green, bottom left blue, bottom right
         white, a white arrow pointing up, a grey going from black to white
      2. the brightness at 100, 60, 20 and 100 %, 2 s each
      3. pictures as fast as the panel takes them, for 10 s
      then let go: the panel shows its own animation again

  lp-panel test --port COM5 --kind majesty
      a port and a kind of your choosing (majesty or hongtai)

  lp-panel test --seconds 30
      a longer run of pictures

What it prints, and what the panel showed at each step, tells whether the driver is right.

jpeg62.dll is libjpeg-turbo (LICENSE-libjpeg-turbo.md, README-libjpeg.ijg); lp-panel is
Libre Panel's, under the GPL (LICENSE-libre-panel.txt).
